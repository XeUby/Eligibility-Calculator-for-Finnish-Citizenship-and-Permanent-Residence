Finland eligibility estimator

Estimate the residence-time part of Finnish citizenship and permanent-residence applications. Calculations run privately in this browser; no data is sent or stored.

Not legal advice or an application decision. This tool covers selected residence-time rules only. Migri decides every application and can assess facts this site cannot verify.

1. Residence-permit history

Add every uninterrupted permit period relevant to your calculation.

A = continuous permit, B = temporary permit, P = permanent permit. Permit end dates are included.

2. Trips outside Finland

Optional, but add trips during the period you want assessed.

The day you leave Finland and the day you return count as residence days, following Migri’s published guidance.

3. Choose the routes to estimate

The 5-year route requires the statutory language qualification. Special routes are not modelled.

These paths apply to applications submitted on or after 8 January 2026.
